Vehicle art for Cars & Coffee.

The 30 top-down vehicles in assets/cars.png are original artwork
commissioned for this project by Jason Fields (Fasonista), cut from the
"Cars & Coffee Vehicle Sprite Sheet, 30 unique vehicles, top-down,
north facing" and reduced to game resolution.

Earlier builds of this game shipped the "Road To Rage" vehicle pack by
TMD Studios (tmdstudios.wordpress.com). That pack is no longer used and
none of its art remains in this build. The attribution is kept here as
a record of what previous builds contained.
